Binance 3-Hour 50% Surge Research

v6.0 adds automatic fresh confirmation and a sealed continuous executable-entry backtest. Parameters are frozen before the historical results are opened.

A qualifying event is the earliest later-minute high at least 50% above a prior-minute low within a conservative three-hour rolling window. The price only needs to touch the threshold.

“Saleable” means at least 500 quote units of seller-initiated trades executed at any price during the five minutes after the exact crossing trade. It does not guarantee a fill at the threshold.

Step 1 — Queue discovery scan

Leave both dates blank for the latest 60 days. For a fresh earlier round, enter both dates; maximum span 180 days.

Step 2 — Build positive-event archive

Step 3 — Build matched controls

Controls use the same symbol and historical split, prefer the same clock time, and exclude windows close to any other 50% crossing. Do not open the sealed-test package before rules are fixed.

Step 4 — Build ten-day context

For the already analysed 63-event job, choose exploratory only. Fresh staged mode is only valid for a newly collected historical job whose later packages have not been opened.

Step 5 — Build baseline-aligned context

For the existing 63-event job, choose exploratory. Fresh staged mode requires a new explicit historical scan ending on or before 22 May 2026 and matched controls built with the 180-minute horizon included.

Step 6 — Confirm H1 on fresh history

The app opens discovery, validation and sealed packages automatically only after the rule and criteria are fixed in code. A failed result does not unlock Step 7.

Step 7 — Run sealed continuous backtest

This evaluates every completed one-minute bar and reconstructs fills from Binance aggregate trades. It can be lengthy. Do not change dates or parameters after seeing results.
