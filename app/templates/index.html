Binance 3-Hour 50% Surge Research

Scans the previous 60 completed UTC days across currently TRADING Binance Spot pairs. “Tradeable” is exchange-level, not confirmation that a particular UK account may trade the pair.

A qualifying event is the earliest later-minute high at least 50% above a prior-minute low within a conservative three-hour rolling window. The price only needs to touch the threshold. Same-minute low-to-high moves are excluded because one-minute bars cannot prove trade order.

“Saleable” means at least 500 quote units of seller-initiated trades executed at any price during the five minutes after the exact crossing trade. It does not require the price to remain 50% higher and does not reconstruct historical order-book depth.
