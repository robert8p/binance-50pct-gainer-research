Binance 8-Hour 50% Surge Research

v8.0 tests the frozen volatility-reversal precursor using non-event baselines selected by the same eight-hour rolling-minimum algorithm as genuine surge events.

A qualifying event is the earliest later-minute high at least 50% above a prior-minute low within a conservative eight-hour rolling window. The price only needs to touch the threshold.

“Saleable” means at least 500 quote units of seller-initiated trades executed at any price during the five minutes after the exact crossing trade. It does not guarantee a fill at the threshold.

Leave both dates blank for the latest 60 days. For a fresh earlier round, enter both dates; maximum span 180 days.

Step 3 — Build matched controls

Controls use the same symbol and historical split, prefer the same clock time, and exclude windows close to any other 50% crossing. Do not open the sealed-test package before rules are fixed.

Step 4 — Build ten-day context

V7 only lists eight-hour matched-control jobs. Use exploratory for opened data; use fresh staged only for a genuinely untouched historical scan.

Step 5 — Build baseline-aligned context

Fresh staged mode requires a new V7 scan ending on or before 1 January 2026 and matched controls containing the mandatory 480-minute horizon. Prior three-hour jobs are deliberately excluded.

Step 6 — Confirm H3 with algorithmic local-low controls

Controls use the scanner’s identical baseline rule: the latest minimum low in the prior 479 minutes. Any control that subsequently rises 50% within eight hours is rejected. Results are also split by event duration band.

Step 7 — Run sealed continuous backtest

This evaluates H3 after every completed one-minute bar, arms the coin for eight hours, and then requires the unchanged late-momentum confirmation. Fills are reconstructed from Binance aggregate trades. Do not change dates or parameters after seeing results.
