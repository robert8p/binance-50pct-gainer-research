Binance ChatGPT Research Exporter

v10.0 keeps the app in its proper role: it collects, cleans, matches and partitions the evidence. ChatGPT—not the app—will generate features, search for patterns and formulate candidate rules.

V9’s hard-coded momentum backtest is retired and disabled. V10 contains no preferred predictor, signal threshold, profit target or trading rule.

Step 1 — Build a fresh 8-hour event scan

This is a 180-day historical window. Use a period whose raw event/control evidence has not previously been examined.

Step 2 — Create neutral ChatGPT evidence packages

The exporter produces raw Parquet data, metadata and audit files. It does not calculate or rank predictive features.

Correct research sequence

First: download CHATGPT_RESEARCH_INDEX.zip and DISCOVERY_UPLOAD_TO_CHATGPT.zip and upload both to ChatGPT.

Keep closed: VALIDATION_DO_NOT_OPEN.zip and SEALED_TEST_DO_NOT_OPEN.zip until ChatGPT has completed blank-canvas discovery and frozen candidate rules and acceptance criteria.
