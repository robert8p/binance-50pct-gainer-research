Binance Exact Entry Validation

V12.0.0 continuously tests the two frozen 50% research triggers on untouched 2025 validation evidence using Binance aggregate-trade execution proxies.

No stop-loss is used. The primary exit is +15% or 24 hours, whichever occurs first. The app does not access the separately reserved July–October 2025 sealed period.

Frozen execution

Entry: first buyer-initiated aggregate trades after the signal, accumulated to 500 quote units within 60 seconds.

Exit: first seller-initiated aggregate trades after +15% is touched, or after 24 hours if it is not touched.

Diagnostics: exact 8h, 24h and 32h maximum favourable/adverse excursion and +10%, +15%, +25% and +50% target hits.
