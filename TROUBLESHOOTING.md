# Troubleshooting — V7.0.0

## Dashboard fails after upgrade

Run `supabase/migrate_v6_to_v7.sql` in Supabase, then redeploy both Render services. The V7 dashboard expects the eight-hour defaults and V7 protocol identifiers.

## Old scans do not appear in dropdowns

This is intentional. V7 excludes scans whose event definition is the old three-hour rule. Queue a new scan after upgrading.

A valid V7 source scan must show:

```text
Event definition: v7_rolling_8h
Window: 480 minutes
```

## Matched-control job is rejected

Confirm that:

- the source scan is a completed V7 eight-hour scan;
- decision horizons include `480`;
- contamination protection is 480 minutes before and after the control anchor;
- the source scan has saleable events.

## Fresh baseline-context job is rejected

Fresh staged mode requires:

- a completed V7 matched-control job;
- a source scan with explicit historical dates;
- a source scan ending no later than **1 January 2026**;
- discovery, validation and sealed packages generated by the V7 workflow.

The recommended fresh period is **1 November 2025–1 January 2026**, end exclusive.

## Confirmation job fails

Confirm the source baseline-context job:

- completed successfully;
- used `fresh_staged` mode;
- contains discovery, validation and sealed packages;
- came from an eligible V7 scan ending no later than 1 January 2026.

Do not use the previously opened January–February or May–July datasets as fresh V7 confirmation evidence.

## Backtest button has no selectable job

Step 7 only lists completed V7 Step 6 jobs whose decision is **PASS**. A failed confirmation deliberately locks the backtest.

## Backtest rejects the dates

The backtest must:

- start on or after the confirmation source scan's exclusive end date;
- end no later than 22 May 2026;
- use a non-empty period;
- be linked to a passing V7 confirmation.

The recommended sealed dates remain **1 March–22 May 2026** after the November–December 2025 confirmation scan.

## Why the trade hold still says three hours

This is intentional. V7 broadens the target-event and precursor-to-continuation windows to eight hours. It does not silently change the separately frozen execution rule. The maximum trade hold remains three hours unless a future preregistered research round explicitly changes it.

## Backtest is slow

This is expected. It evaluates every completed minute across the current canonical Binance Spot universe and downloads official daily archives. The worker processes symbols sequentially to control disk usage.

Do not queue a duplicate job. Refresh the dashboard manually to view progress.

## Many entry or exit fills fail

This is a research result, not necessarily a software failure. The app requires historical aggressive-side executions to prove a 500-quote-unit entry and full exit. Check `aggregate_trade_coverage.csv` to distinguish missing archives from insufficient executed liquidity.

## Worker restarts

Running jobs are automatically requeued. To keep the 10 GB disk bounded, V7 removes per-symbol cache files after processing, so a restarted long backtest may redownload some files.
